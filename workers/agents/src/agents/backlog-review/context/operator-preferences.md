# Operator preferences (trusted, changed only by a reviewed pull request)

- Do not triage new issues: issues nobody accepted yet belong to triage.
- Status ping on an issue waiting for its author or assignee: after 7 days without activity.
- Unassign with `HELP_WANTED` after a further 7 days with no answer to the status question.
- Re-ping a maintainer who has not answered: after 5 days.
- Cooldown for the same kind of comment on one issue: 5 days.
- More than 3 open pull requests plus assignments by one person: ask them to finish those first.
- Merging and approving pull requests stay with maintainers; issues only in this version.
- Comments carry no signature and no mention of Custodes; the operator posts them as their own
  after review.
